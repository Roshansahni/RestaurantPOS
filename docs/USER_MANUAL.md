# User Manual

Sign in with the username and password your admin gave you. Your role decides which screens appear in the top bar.

## Cashier
**Billing:** tap dishes to add them; use +/− to change quantity. Choose Dine-in (pick a free table), Takeaway or Online. Optionally pick a customer and enter a discount in rupees. Press a Pay button (Cash, UPI, Card, Other). The receipt appears with GST split; press Print receipt. Payments are simulated: no money is processed by the app.
**Customers:** add a customer (name and phone) and search by name or phone.

## Kitchen staff
**Kitchen:** columns show New, Preparing and Ready orders (updates instantly; a red banner means it is reconnecting and checking every 5 seconds). Press Start, Mark ready, then Complete. Orders older than 15 minutes show a red edge.

## Manager (and admin)
**Dashboard:** revenue, orders, popular dishes, recent payments, low-stock list, 14-day chart; export CSV or PDF.
**Menu:** add, edit or delete dishes; untick "Available" to hide a dish from billing.
**Inventory:** add ingredients with a reorder level; "Add stock" records a purchase, "Use" records consumption. Low items are flagged.
**Tables:** add tables and set Available / Occupied / Reserved.
**Team:** add employees and mark attendance.

## Admin
**Settings:** restaurant name, address, GSTIN and receipt footer (printed on receipts); create staff accounts, change roles, deactivate accounts. Everyone can change their own password here.
